# Thoughtline 隐私说明与支持 / Privacy & Support

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-15

适用 / Scope: Thoughtline 0.0.38 Microsoft Store candidate. Publisher: NocodeMrli. Store ID: 9PK55N0ZM4TB.

## 中文

Thoughtline是一款在电脑本地保存念头的桌面工具。无需注册Thoughtline账号。本说明描述当前版本的实际数据处理方式。

### 在本地处理哪些信息

应用会在本地保存你输入的名称、正文、分组、标签、草稿、提醒时间，以及界面语言、外观和快捷键等设置。记录还包含创建/修改时间与本地数据维护所需的信息。你可以在“存储与设置”中查看或选择数据目录、导出念头或备份。用户选择的自定义目录或外部备份也可能位于应用目录之外。

### 数据如何使用和共享

这些内容用于展示、搜索、组织和恢复你的念头，以及提供提醒。当前版本没有Thoughtline云同步、开发者运营的账号系统、应用内广告或行为分析服务；应用不会主动把念头正文上传到开发者服务器。

你主动点击复制时，正文会写入系统剪贴板；拖放、导出或备份会把内容交给你指定的目标程序或位置。剪贴板历史/同步、通知中心、目标应用以及你自行使用的云盘可能由Windows或第三方处理，相应行为受其设置与政策约束。系统通知可能显示念头标题和正文片段，你可以在应用或Windows设置中调整提醒和通知。

微软商店的购买、下载、授权和更新由微软处理。Thoughtline本身不接收银行卡、税务或商店付款信息。主动打开作者主页、发送反馈邮件时，由浏览器、邮件服务及相应网站处理网络请求；如果你把内容或截图发给开发者，开发者会收到你主动提供的资料，用于回复和排查问题，请先脱敏。

### 你的控制与本地安全

你可以在应用中查看、修改、删除念头；最近删除中的记录仍可能存在，彻底删除不会自动清除你过去导出的备份。外部备份、自选目录和系统备份需由你自行管理。卸载前可先备份需要保留的内容；卸载不保证清除你主动保存到其他位置的文件。

当前本地文件没有应用级端到端加密或密码保险箱保护。请使用自己的系统账户、设备锁定、磁盘保护和妥善的备份管理来保护数据。请不要把银行卡密码等高度敏感资料当作普通便签保存。

本说明会随实际功能变化更新。使用问题或隐私问题请联系：lhw4553@126.com。反馈时不必提供身份证、银行卡、密码或完整私人念头库。

## English

Thoughtline is a desktop tool that keeps notes locally on your computer. A Thoughtline account is not required. This notice describes the current version's data handling.

### Information processed locally

The app stores the titles, text, groups, tags, drafts and reminder times you enter, together with settings such as language, appearance and keyboard shortcuts. Records also include creation/modification times and information needed to maintain local data. Storage & settings lets you inspect or choose the data location and export notes or backups. Custom folders and exported backups may exist outside the app's own directory.

### Use and sharing

Local information is used to display, search, organize and restore your notes and provide reminders. This version has no Thoughtline cloud synchronization, developer-operated account system, in-app advertising or behavioral analytics service. It does not intentionally upload note text to a developer server.

When you choose Copy, text is written to the system clipboard. Dragging, exporting and backing up transfer content to an app or location you choose. Clipboard history/synchronization, Windows notifications, destination apps and cloud folders you use may be handled by Windows or third parties under their own settings and policies. Notifications may display a note title and excerpt; reminders and system notification settings are under your control.

Microsoft handles Store purchases, downloads, licensing and updates. Thoughtline itself does not receive your payment card, tax or Store payment information. Opening the author's page or sending feedback uses your browser, email provider and the relevant external services. If you send the developer content or screenshots, the developer receives what you voluntarily provide to answer and investigate your request. Please remove private information first.

### Your controls and local security

You can view, edit and delete notes. Items in Recently deleted may still be retained. Permanent deletion does not remove backups you previously exported; custom folders, external copies and system backups must be managed separately. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your notes. Uninstalling does not guarantee removal of files you saved elsewhere.

Local files are not protected by an app-level end-to-end encryption or password-vault feature. Protect them with your system account, device lock, disk protection and careful backup management. Do not use ordinary notes to store highly sensitive information such as payment card passwords.

This notice will be updated when the app's actual functionality changes. For support or privacy questions, contact lhw4553@126.com. Do not send identity documents, payment details, passwords or your entire private note database.
