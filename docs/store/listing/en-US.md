## Description
Thoughtline is a lightweight desktop note app for passing ideas, work notes, and text you use every day.

Move your pointer to the top edge of the screen to reveal a vine and its notes. Move away to tuck them back and return to your work. Use colored clips to organize notes, stack related papers, and find what you need by group, tag, or date.

• Capture quickly: create a note with a keyboard shortcut or the paper's right-click menu, and keep unfinished text as a draft.
• Click to copy: take a note's full text with one click, or drag it into a chat or editor that accepts text drops. Nothing is sent automatically.
• Drag to stack: put related papers on one clip, turn pages, or pull out a single paper.
• Organize with groups and tags: sort notes by project, topic, or purpose, and combine filters to focus your view.
• Revisit in the calendar: browse original creation dates and search note titles, text, or groups.
• Set a one-time reminder: allow system notifications and keep the app running to receive reminders.
• Make it yours: switch between English and Chinese, light and dark themes, and vine or other rope styles.
• Keep notes locally: no Thoughtline account is required, and local backups are available.

Use Thoughtline to capture ideas, keep meeting leads close, and reuse replies or prompts. The current Store candidate targets x64 devices running Windows 10 version 2004 or later.

## Features
- Reveal notes at the top edge and tuck them away when the pointer leaves
- Copy full note text with a click or drag text into compatible apps
- Stack papers by dragging, turn pages, and split out individual notes
- Organize and combine filters with groups and tags
- Browse notes in a calendar, search, and set one-time reminders
- English and Chinese UI, light and dark themes, and rope styles
- Local notes and backups without an app account
